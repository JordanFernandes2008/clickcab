## Rules for adding to this folder

- **Commercial use must be permitted.** Google Images results are not
  licensed by virtue of being findable there.
- **Prefer CC0 / public domain**, which needs no credit. CC-BY and CC-BY-SA
  are usable but oblige the site to carry a visible attribution line.
- Record every non-original image in the table above.
