Privacy Policy

What we collect when you book a cab with us, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it.

Last updated: CLIENT TO CONFIRM: date this policy is published
This notice is written to meet the requirements of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”).

1. Who we are

This website is operated by CLIENT TO CONFIRM: full registered business name, e.g. Click Cabs Pvt Ltd (“Click Cabs”, “we”, “us”). Under the DPDP Act we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described below, which means we decide why and how it is processed.

Contact: +91 97022 90804 · info@clickcabs.in CLIENT TO CONFIRM: official email address

2. What personal data we collect

When you use a booking form on this website we collect:

  • Your name — so the driver knows who they are meeting.
  • Your mobile number — to confirm the booking and for the driver to reach you.
  • Your email address — optional. Given only if you want a written confirmation.
  • Trip details — pickup and drop locations, date, time, number of passengers, vehicle or package chosen, and any notes you add.
  • A booking reference we generate, and the page you booked from.

We do not ask for or store payment card details, government identity numbers, or any of the categories the DPDP Act treats as requiring additional care, through this website.

If you continue the booking on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled by WhatsApp under their own privacy policy.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, quote a fare and arrange the vehicle.
  • To contact you about that specific booking — confirmation, driver details, changes.
  • To keep a record of bookings for our own accounts and for resolving disputes.

We do not use your details for marketing unless you have separately asked us to, and we do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

4. Your consent, and how to withdraw it

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give when you submit a booking form. Submitting the form means you agree to us contacting you about that booking.

You can withdraw that consent at any time by calling or emailing us using the contact details in section 1. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it. If you withdraw it we will stop processing your data and delete it, unless we are required by law to keep a record of a completed journey.

5. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary with:

  • The driver or vendor assigned to your trip — your name, mobile number and pickup point, so they can complete the journey.
  • Google (Firebase) — our booking records are stored on Google’s Firebase platform, which acts as our Data Processor.
  • Brevo — used only to deliver your confirmation email, if you gave an email address.

These providers process data on our instructions only. We do not otherwise transfer your data outside India except as part of these providers’ normal operation.

6. How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for CLIENT TO CONFIRM: retention period, e.g. 24 months after the journey, then deleted. Enquiries that never became bookings are deleted sooner. Under the DPDP Act we must erase personal data once the purpose it was collected for is served, unless a law requires us to keep it.

7. How we protect it

Booking records are held in Google Firebase with access rules that allow the public to submit a booking but only authorised Click Cabs staff to read one. Staff access requires an individual account and password. Our website is served over HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

8. Your rights

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access — ask what personal data of yours we hold and who we have shared it with.
  • Correction and completion — have inaccurate or incomplete data fixed.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data where we no longer need it.
  • Grievance redressal — complain to us and get a response, before going further.
  • Nomination — nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. We will respond within CLIENT TO CONFIRM: response window, commonly 30 days.

10. Children

Our services are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect the personal data of anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian, as the DPDP Act requires. If you believe a child has given us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

11. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store a small amount of data locally to remember things like a dismissed offer banner; that stays on your device and is never sent to us.
